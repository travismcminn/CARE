Our Mission

Real financial relief. Real community impact.

Community Assistance & Relief Effort (C.A.R.E.) is a Missouri nonprofit organization with its IRS 501(c)(3) application pending, currently serving Fredericktown, Missouri, and surrounding communities by helping individuals and families experiencing financial hardship.

Our mission is simple: bring together generous people in our community and turn their contributions into direct, meaningful financial assistance for those facing difficult circumstances.

We focus especially on the holiday season, when financial pressure can make an already challenging situation even harder. Our long-term vision is to extend this same neighbor-to-neighbor support to additional communities as our fundraising capacity grows.

  • Direct financial assistance
  • Confidential, referral-based selection
  • Thoughtful review and board oversight
  • Transparency without sacrificing privacy

Our Guiding Principles

Compassion with accountability.

C.A.R.E. is built around a simple, personal approach to helping others, while protecting dignity and respecting the generosity of every donor.

🔒

Privacy & Dignity

We protect the identities of donors and recipients in public communications. Nobody should have to sacrifice their dignity to receive help.

🤝

Trusted Referrals

Potential recipients are identified through referrals from community members, churches, and trusted local organizations.

⚖️

Board Oversight

Referrals are reviewed using established charitable criteria. Assistance decisions are subject to board oversight and available funds.

💵

Financial Relief

Our focus is on providing financial assistance that addresses genuine needs, rather than organizing gift or merchandise drives.

💬

Personal Connection

We seek to understand each referred family's circumstances and needs before determining appropriate assistance.

📋

Donor Transparency

Donors can receive updates on how funds were used and the impact created, without publicly identifying those receiving assistance.

How C.A.R.E. Works

A thoughtful approach to giving.

Every situation is different. Our process is designed to help us understand real needs and provide meaningful assistance responsibly.

01

Community Referral

A trusted individual, church, or community organization refers someone experiencing hardship.

02

Personal Conversation

We privately connect with the individual or family to understand their circumstances and financial needs.

03

Review & Assistance

The request is evaluated through our review process. Approved assistance is provided based on documented need and available resources.

Know Someone Who Needs Help?

Sometimes the people who need help most never ask.

C.A.R.E. accepts confidential referrals from community members, churches, and local organizations who know individuals or families facing genuine financial hardship.

Referrals are not public applications. Each situation is reviewed privately, and assistance is never guaranteed. Please do not share sensitive personal or financial details through ordinary email.
