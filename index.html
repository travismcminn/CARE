Who We Are

Because everyone needs a helping hand sometimes.

Community Assistance & Relief Effort, better known as C.A.R.E., is a Missouri-based nonprofit organization created with one simple belief: when neighbors come together, we can make difficult times a little easier.

Life can change in an instant. An unexpected expense, a job loss, a medical emergency, or a difficult holiday season can leave individuals and families struggling to make ends meet.

Our mission is to turn generosity into meaningful assistance by connecting community support with people who need it most.

  • Compassion without judgment
  • Practical assistance for real needs
  • Responsible stewardship of donations
  • Stronger, more connected communities

How We Hope to Help

Support where it matters most.

C.A.R.E. focuses on practical, compassionate assistance that can help families overcome immediate challenges and find stability.

🛒

Food & Groceries

Helping individuals and families access groceries and essential food items when their budgets fall short.

🏠

Housing & Utilities

Assistance with qualifying urgent housing and utility needs to help families maintain a safe, stable home.

🎁

Holiday Assistance

Helping bring comfort, essentials, and a little extra joy to families during the holiday season.

💚

Emergency Relief

Responding to unexpected financial hardships and urgent needs when resources are available.

🧺

Everyday Essentials

Supporting access to necessary household items, personal care supplies, and other basic necessities.

🤝

Community Partnerships

Bringing donors, volunteers, businesses, and neighbors together to create a stronger local support network.

Assistance is subject to available funding, eligibility criteria, and review. Listing a program does not guarantee financial assistance.

We're Here for Our Community

Going through a difficult time?

Asking for help takes courage. If you or someone you know is facing financial hardship, contact C.A.R.E. to ask about our assistance process and available resources.

Ask About Assistance →

C.A.R.E. is not an emergency response service. Assistance requests are reviewed based on available resources and established criteria. For an immediate emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service.
