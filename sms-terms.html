SMS Terms & Conditions

Terms for C.A.R.E. Community Impact Updates text messages.

Effective date: October 28, 2026

These SMS Terms & Conditions apply to the voluntary C.A.R.E. Community Impact Updates text messaging program operated by Community Assistance & Relief Effort (C.A.R.E.), a Missouri nonprofit organization.

1. Program description

Subscribers may receive recurring text messages about C.A.R.E.'s fundraising campaigns, donation milestones, community activities, and general or anonymized accounts of charitable assistance. We do not disclose the identities or confidential circumstances of assistance recipients in our SMS updates.

2. How to join

Participation is optional. You may opt in by texting CARE to (573) 273-6036, or through a C.A.R.E. website signup form that expressly requests SMS consent when available. We will not automatically enroll donors, assistance applicants, or other contacts. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of donating, receiving assistance, purchasing goods or services, or using our website.

3. Frequency and costs

We expect to send up to four messages per month. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan. C.A.R.E. does not charge a subscription fee for these updates.

4. Unsubscribe

Reply STOP to any program message, or text STOP to (573) 273-6036, to unsubscribe. You may receive a final confirmation of your opt-out. After opting out, you will not receive further program messages unless you subscribe again.

5. Help and support

Reply HELP to any program message or text HELP to (573) 273-6036. You can also email carehelps.org@gmail.com or call (573) 576-9305.

6. Delivery

Message delivery depends on wireless networks, device availability, and service-provider systems. Delivery is not guaranteed, and wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

7. Privacy and consent

Our use of mobile numbers and SMS consent records is described in our Privacy Policy. SMS consent is separate from any other agreement or transaction with C.A.R.E. We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or consent records with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

8. Changes

We may update these terms as the program evolves. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date if material changes are made.
