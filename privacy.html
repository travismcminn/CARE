Privacy Policy

How C.A.R.E. protects your information, including SMS signup and consent data.

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Community Assistance & Relief Effort (C.A.R.E.) respects the privacy of donors, subscribers, assistance applicants, and community members. This policy explains the information we collect through our website and our voluntary text messaging program, how we use it, and when service providers may process it on our behalf.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect contact information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and mobile phone number; SMS signup and consent records; communications and requests you send us; and donation transaction details supplied by our payment processor. Assistance referrals may contain sensitive information about a person's circumstances, which we treat as confidential.

2. How we use information

We use information to administer donations and charitable assistance, respond to inquiries, maintain required organizational and accounting records, and send fundraising progress and community impact updates only to people who have affirmatively subscribed to SMS messages. Donating or requesting assistance does not automatically subscribe you to text messages.

3. SMS privacy commitments

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or SMS consent records with third parties or affiliates for their independent marketing or promotional purposes. SMS information is used to deliver requested updates, manage subscriptions and opt-outs, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable obligations.

We use messaging service providers, including SimpleTexting, to process subscriber information and deliver messages on C.A.R.E.'s behalf. Such providers may access information only as needed to provide their services and meet applicable legal requirements; this is not permission for them to use your SMS consent for independent marketing.

4. Other service providers

We may use third-party services for donations, forms, bookkeeping, website hosting, and administrative operations. These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services. Payment information is processed through our payment providers rather than being collected directly in our website's HTML.

5. Assistance-recipient confidentiality

We seek to protect the dignity and privacy of people receiving assistance. Public updates and SMS messages will not identify recipients without an appropriate, separate authorization. Even when describing a situation anonymously, we review details to reduce the risk that someone could be identified in a small community.

6. Retention and security

We retain records as reasonably necessary for program administration, legal compliance, financial accountability, and resolving inquiries. We take reasonable steps to restrict access to sensitive information, but no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may stop SMS updates at any time by replying STOP to a message or texting STOP to (573) 273-6036. For help, reply HELP. You can contact us to ask questions about your information or request an update or deletion, subject to records we are required to retain.

8. SMS terms

For program frequency, pricing disclosures, and subscription instructions, read our SMS Terms & Conditions.
